DoctorClinic Account Deletion

DoctorClinic respects your privacy. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email with the subject Delete My Account to: snowyflutterdev@gmail.com

Include These Details

  • Full Name
  • Registered Email Address
  • Phone Number (optional)

Data Deleted

  • User profile
  • Appointments linked to account
  • Saved preferences
  • Uploaded personal data

Data That May Be Retained

  • Legal compliance records
  • Fraud prevention logs
  • Security records

Processing Time

Requests are usually processed within 7–30 business days.
